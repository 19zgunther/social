# Double down on what you have

- Animated pixel reactions — custom 64×64 emoji that play a short loop when reacted (bounce, sparkle, explode into confetti).
- Stick-figure posts — ship the animator WIP as a first-class post type: tiny looping clips in the feed, not video dumps.
- Poll → event pipeline — Who's free Saturday? poll that one-taps into a thread event with RSVPs.
- Pool rematches & leaderboards — per-friend W/L, best shot of the week still frame shared to the feed.
- Congrats everywhere — more magic words / emoji that trigger little overlays (rain, fireworks, slow clap).

# Shared-moment social (vs broadcast)

- Co-authored posts — two friends both edit one photo/caption before it goes live.
- Together albums — auto-group photos taken near the same time from people in a thread (opt-in).
- Pass-the-camera — group chain: each person adds one frame; result is a short comic/story strip.
- Shared doodle on a photo — everyone in the thread can draw on the same image for 60s.

# Playful constraints (makes it feel like a game)

- Daily prompt — one silly theme for the friend circle (show your desk mess); no public explore, just your people.
- Post with a timer — disappears or locks after N hours (Stories energy without the Instagram clone feel).
- Caption lottery — friends submit captions; author (or a poll) picks the winner.
- Blind reactions first — see others' reactions only after you react.

# Presence & friendship texture

- Mood / status stickers on the presence ring (coding, cooking, do not @ me).
- Friend anniversaries — friends since milestones with a one-tap balloon post.
- Watch party — sync a stick-figure clip or album in a thread while on a call.

# Weird-but-sticky differentiators

- Soundbites — 3-second voice notes as reactions (not full voice chat).
- Inside-joke packs — private emoji + sticker sets scoped to a group thread.
- Map of vibes (not locations) — friends place a pin on a silly mood map for the day.
- Trade posts — I'll post if you post challenges between two friends.
